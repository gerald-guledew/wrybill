# Code of Conduct

Wrybill is built by people from different places, backgrounds and levels of experience, and we want everyone to feel welcome here. This code applies to everyone who takes part, wherever they do it: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and anywhere else someone represents the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and make it specific and useful.
- Disagree well. Make your case clearly, listen to the other side, and accept the decision once it's made.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, body or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or carrying on after you've been asked to stop.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, or you see someone breaking this Code of Conduct, please tell the maintainer, Gerald Guledew, privately. Email him at the address on his GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer looks into every report and responds in proportion to what happened: a quiet word, a warning, removing comments or contributions, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project. Nobody will face consequences for reporting in good faith.

This code was written for Wrybill in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
